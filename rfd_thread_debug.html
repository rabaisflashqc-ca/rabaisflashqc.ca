chrisli779 wrote: ↑ question: is this router good enough for a 3000 sq house which I'd like to cover also the area in the backyard and driveway or should I get a mesh router setup?
As others have noted, it likely won't be enough for a 3000 sq ft home.

My home is smaller and in order to get sufficient coverage I have several TP-link routers hardwired to each other and use TP-link's EasyMesh that syncs the wireless network settings from the main router and is supposed to let my devices move / switch between the faster router as I move around the house.

This will be my new setup once I get the new Archer 550:
Basement: TP-Link Archer 550 Pro connected via 10G port to Bell Home Hub (3Gb fiber)
Ground floor: TP-Link Tri-Band WiFi 6E Router (Archer AXE75): connected to the 550 Pro via a 1Gb port (so this will be my weak link; 6GHz wifi devices will cap at 1Gb speeds).
Upstairs: new TP-Link Archer 550 connected to the 550 Pro via 2.5G port.

This should give me strong 6GHz connections throughout the house and hopefully into the backyard. I do find that 5GHz and 6GHz do drop off quite a bit the further you get from the router / has trouble through walls. Perhaps there are issues with TP-Link routers, but I suspect most 6GHz signals from most routers would suffer the same problems. I previously had decent ASUS routers that also struggled with 5GHz dropping off.
